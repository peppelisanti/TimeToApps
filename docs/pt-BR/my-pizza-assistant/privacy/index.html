Política de Privacidade

my pizza assistant · Última atualização: 10 de novembro de 2026

Este documento está disponível apenas em inglês por enquanto. Ler a versão em inglês

This privacy policy describes how personal data of users of the mobile application my pizza assistant (the “App”), for iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play), is processed.

1. Summary

There is no account to create. The recipes you save (doughs, timers, ingredients, notes, ratings and photos) are stored on your device; on iOS they are also synced between your own devices through your private iCloud space, while on Android they stay on the device. We do not receive them and we cannot read them. The App is free and shows ads (Google AdMob); we use Firebase for usage analytics, crash reports and remote settings. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data controller

The data controller is Giuseppe Lisanti, residing in Italy. Contact: support.timetoapps@gmail.com

The App does not run its own backend: the recipes you create are never transmitted to systems controlled by the controller.

3. Recipes, photos and iCloud

You may save doughs with a name, quantities, times, notes, a rating and a photo. On iOS, this data is stored on your device and in your private iCloud space (Apple CloudKit), so it stays in sync across the devices signed in with your Apple ID; it is also included in your device backups according to your Apple settings. If iCloud is turned off, the data stays on the device only. On Android, it is stored only on your device, with no sync, and it may be included in Google’s device backup linked to your Google account, according to your Android settings. We do not have access to your iCloud data or to your device backups.

The App uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo of your pizza to attach to a recipe, and the system photo picker when you choose one from your library: it receives only the photo you select and does not read the rest of your library. The photo is resized and stored with that recipe.

4. Timers, notifications and widgets

Timer reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. On iOS, the timer can also appear in a Live Activity, on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and in a Home Screen widget; on Android, as an ongoing notification. All of this happens on your device and does not pass through our servers.

5. Data collected by third-party services

The App collects data solely through third-party SDKs (Google AdMob, Google UMP and Firebase, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd) for advertising, aggregate usage analytics, diagnostics and remote settings. No name, email, address, phone number, contacts, precise GPS location, recipe content, photos, health or financial data is ever sent to them.

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). Data: advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android), truncated IP address, device information, ad interactions. Purpose: display banner ads and, where enabled, full-screen ads (personalised or non-personalised based on your consent). Legal basis: consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland) / legitimate interest (other regions).
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics). Data: pseudonymous installation identifier, App usage events (app opens, visited screens, key actions such as saving a recipe or starting a timer, with technical details such as the hydration or whether a photo was attached, never the photo or the text), device model, operating system version, language, coarse country-level region. Purpose: understand how the App is used in order to improve it. Legal basis: consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland).
  • Diagnostics and crashes (Firebase Crashlytics). Data: crash logs, stack traces, device model, operating system version, pseudonymous installation identifier. Purpose: identify and fix malfunctions. Legal basis: legitimate interest of the controller in maintaining a working App.
  • Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config). Data: pseudonymous installation identifier, App version, device and language information. Purpose: adjust some settings without a new release, such as where and how often ads appear. Legal basis: legitimate interest of the controller.

Technical note: on iOS the App uses FirebaseAnalyticsWithoutAdIdSupport, a build of Analytics that never collects the IDFA. Analytics events are therefore pseudonymous and are not linked to the advertising identifier.

6. Consent management (GDPR / ePrivacy)

On first launch, if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows a consent form provided by Google UMP (User Messaging Platform). Through that form you can:

  • accept or decline personalised advertising;
  • accept or decline usage analytics;
  • review the full list of Google’s advertising partners.

If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised, and Firebase Analytics will not collect usage events (“Consent Mode” with all parameters denied).

You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy → Manage consent inside the App.

7. Tracking (iOS) and advertising ID (Android)

Separately from GDPR consent, iOS shows Apple’s “App Tracking Transparency” prompt to ask whether you want to allow the App to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. This prompt controls access to the IDFA. If you decline, the App and Google AdMob will not be able to use the IDFA for measurement or personalisation, and you will only receive non-personalised, contextual ads. You can change your choice under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, ads use the Android advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on the device).

8. Data retention

  • Recipes, photos and timers: kept until you delete them in the App. On iOS, deleting the App does not delete the copy in your iCloud: you can remove it under iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage. On Android, deleting the App deletes them from the device; any Google backup follows your Google account settings.
  • Advertising data (AdMob): governed by Google’s privacy policy. Data associated with the advertising identifier is retained by Google according to its retention policies (typically up to 60 days for raw data and up to 14 months for aggregated data).
  • Analytics and crash data: retained by Google according to its policies, typically 2 months for event-level data and 90 days for crash data, unless a different retention period is set in the controller’s Firebase project.

9. Recipients of the data

  • Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd (AdMob, Firebase, UMP), with EU establishment in Ireland. Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • The advertising partners listed in the UMP consent form, only active if you consent to personalised advertising.
  • Apple (iCloud) and Google (Android backup) store your recipes on your behalf, under your own account and their privacy policies; the controller has no access to them.

Data is never sold to third parties. The controller has no access to raw data: only aggregated reports are visible through the AdMob and Firebase dashboards.

10. Transfers outside the EU

Google operates servers in the United States and other countries. Transfers are governed by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and, for transfers to the United States, by Google’s adherence to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR (EU 2016/679) you have the right to:

  • access the data concerning you;
  • request its rectification or erasure;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive the data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (via “Manage consent” in Settings);
  • lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority (in Italy: www.garanteprivacy.it).

To exercise your rights, write to support.timetoapps@gmail.com. Your recipes are under your control: you can edit or delete them in the App at any time. The controller does not directly hold the data collected by Google’s SDKs: for deletion of or access to specific data, you may need to contact Google directly through Google’s privacy dashboard.

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age under your local law) and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has used the App without permission, please contact us and we will erase any data tied to their device.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. Changes will be published at this URL and communicated in-App for substantial changes. The date at the top shows the last update.

14. Contact

For any question about privacy or the processing of your data: Giuseppe Lisanti · support.timetoapps@gmail.com
