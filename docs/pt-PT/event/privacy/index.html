Política de Privacidade

Time to Event · Última atualização: 6 de novembro de 2026

Este documento está atualmente disponível apenas em inglês. Ler a versão em inglês

This Privacy Policy explains how Giuseppe Lisanti (“we”, “us”) handles information in the Time to Event app (the “App”).

1. Summary

The App helps you organise a big event (a wedding, a graduation, a trip, a dinner, a party): budget, quotes, payments, the people and companies you work with, documents and the guest list. There is no account to create and no bank connection. What you enter is stored on your device and, through Apple’s iCloud, synced between your own devices; we do not receive it and we cannot read it. If you choose to share an event with someone, it is shared through Apple’s iCloud with the people you invite (see “Sharing”). The free version shows ads (Google AdMob) and we use Firebase for crash reports, usage analytics and remote settings; Pro removes the ads. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data you enter

You may enter event names, dates, budgets, amounts, notes, details about the professionals and suppliers you work with (such as their name, service, phone number and email address), and a guest list: guests’ names, whether they replied, how many people are in each invitation, table assignments, dish choices and food notes (for example allergies or dietary preferences), together with photos and PDF files you attach (for example contracts or receipts). Food notes can reveal health-related or religious information about your guests: enter only what you need and only if you are entitled to keep it. This data is stored on your device and in your private iCloud space (Apple CloudKit) so it stays in sync across your devices, and it is included in your device backups according to your Apple settings. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

3. Camera, photos and contacts

The App asks for access to the camera or the photo library only when you choose to attach a photo to an expense or a document. A photo you attach is resized and stored with that item; the App does not read the rest of your library. If you import guests from your contacts, the App uses Apple’s contact picker: it receives only the contacts you select, and only the details needed (such as the name).

4. Sharing an event

You can share an event with other people through Apple’s iCloud sharing. The owner chooses who is invited and whether each person can edit or only read. People you invite can see everything in that event, including the guest list and attached documents, and (if you let them edit) can change it. Invitations and access are managed by Apple; we do not receive the shared data. You can stop sharing or remove a person at any time from the App. Please share only with people you trust and only data you are entitled to share.

5. Exporting data

With Pro you can export reports (for example a guest and dish summary for a caterer). The file is created on your device and shared only where you choose, through the system share sheet. It does not pass through our servers.

6. Purchases and entitlements

If you buy a subscription or the lifetime unlock, payment is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and manage access to Pro. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and subscription status, and related technical data (such as app version and device type), as described in its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details.

7. Advertising (free version)

If you use the free version, the App shows banner ads via Google AdMob (and related Google Mobile Ads / User Messaging Platform tools). These services may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), coarse location or an IP-derived region, and interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example GDPR/EEA/UK) we ask for your consent first, and on iOS the App asks permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt; tracking is optional and you can refuse or change your choice at any time (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Users with an active Pro entitlement do not see ads.

8. Analytics, diagnostics and remote settings

We use Firebase / Google services (Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config) to measure how the App is used, fix crashes and configure features such as the limits of the free version. They may collect app version, device model, operating system, crash logs, an app-instance identifier and interaction events such as screens viewed. We never send the content you enter (amounts, names, notes, photos). Where consent is required, we request it before enabling ads-related processing.

9. Notifications and widgets

Payment reminders, where available, are local notifications scheduled on your device and do not pass through our servers. If you use a Home Screen widget, the App shares the figures it shows with the widget on your device only.

10. Support

If you contact support by email, we receive your email address, your message, and any diagnostics you choose to include (such as app version and device model). We use them only to answer you.

11. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we process data to provide the App you requested (purchases, support), for our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and working (crash diagnosis, analytics), and, where required, on your consent (ads and tracking). You may withdraw consent from system settings or in the App’s privacy options.

12. Retention

Data you enter remains on your device and in your iCloud until you delete it or uninstall the App (subject to your backups and iCloud settings). Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request. Purchase, advertising and analytics records held by Apple, RevenueCat or Google follow those providers’ retention policies.

13. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

14. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Data you enter can be edited or deleted in the App or by deleting the App. For requests about purchase data, contact us and we will help route the request to Apple or RevenueCat.

15. International transfers

Apple, Google (including AdMob and Firebase), and RevenueCat may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required, appropriate safeguards are applied by those providers.

16. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
