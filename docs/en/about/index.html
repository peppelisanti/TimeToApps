Why four apps

Time to Apps is a small family of apps made by one person, Giuseppe Lisanti. Each app solves a different problem, with its own data and its own screens, so you only install what you need.

Four problems, four apps

Time to Payday

Counting the days to payday and knowing how much you can spend today.

Made for: People paid monthly who want a simple daily budget.

Time to Sub

Subscriptions that renew without you noticing.

Made for: Anyone with several subscriptions who wants to know what they cost and when they renew.

Time to Renovate

A home renovation scattered across quotes, suppliers and invoices.

Made for: Homeowners renovating a house or a flat, who need budget, quotes and documents in one place.

Time to Event

A big event with a fixed date, where budget, suppliers and guests get mixed up.

Made for: Couples, families and hosts planning a wedding, a graduation, a birth or a party.

What they have in common

The same care for a clear, calm interface, no account to create, and the same respect for your data. The apps share some building blocks (design components, purchases, ads and analytics code), but each one has its own data model, screens and logic.

Why not a single app?

Each problem has a different thing to manage: a pay cycle, a list of subscriptions, a renovation site, an event with guests. Putting them in one app would make every part heavier for someone who needs only one.

Made by one person

The apps are designed and developed by me, Giuseppe Lisanti. They are not made from a template or an app generation service. Write to me if you have questions.
